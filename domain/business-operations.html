About This Program

The Business Operations Internship focuses on understanding how organizations manage their daily operations. You will learn how processes, resources, and strategies are aligned to achieve efficiency, productivity, and business growth.



What You Will Learn

Process Management

Designing, analyzing, and improving business processes

Supply Chain Basics

Understanding procurement, logistics, and inventory flow

Operations Strategy

Performance Metrics

Measuring efficiency, productivity, and operational KPIs
