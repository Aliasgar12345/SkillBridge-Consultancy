About This Program

The Financial Analysis Internship is designed to provide hands-on experience in analyzing financial data, understanding business performance, and supporting strategic decision-making. You will work with real financial statements and industry-based case studies.



What You Will Learn

Financial Statements

Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, and Cash Flow analysis

Excel for Finance

Data analysis, formulas, and financial modeling

Budgeting & Forecasting

Planning, cost control, and revenue projections

Business Evaluation

Assessing company performance and financial health

15 Days

30 Days
