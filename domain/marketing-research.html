About This Program

The Marketing Research Internship is designed to help students understand how market data is collected, analyzed, and interpreted. You will learn research techniques used by organizations to make informed marketing and business decisions.



What You Will Learn

Market Study

Understanding market structure, demand, and competition

Survey Design

Creating questionnaires and collecting primary data

Consumer Behavior

Analyzing customer preferences and buying patterns

Data Interpretation

Converting research data into actionable insights
